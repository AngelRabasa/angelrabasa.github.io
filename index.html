About me

I'm learning how capital is priced, and I show the work while I learn it.

I study International Business at ESIC University in Valencia. This term I'm on an Erasmus exchange at Fontys in Eindhoven. Before university I trained in international trade at Capitol Empresa and did an international trade internship at Chuansa Group.

I'm preparing for the CFA Level I exam. Alongside it I write company notes, keep a watchlist and run a small portfolio of my own, so each idea meets real prices.

I've refereed football for the Gandía delegation since 2020. Every week: a decision in a second, then a review on Monday.
